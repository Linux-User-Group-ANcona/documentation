# Join LUGAN! 👋

We are the Linux User Group of Ancona community of researchers, developers, and enthusiasts dedicated to the principles of Free Software and the practice of active development.

If you are ready to learn, contribute to Open Source, and help solve real-world problems with code, here is how you can find us.

---

The fastest way to join the conversation, ask quick questions, and find technical help is through our online chat platforms. Our digital doors are always open!

## 📍 Meet In Person (The Physical Node)
We value face-to-face interaction for workshops, deep dives, and collaborative hacking sessions. This is where our academic and practical spirits meet.

* **Location:** we hold our physical meetings at **UNIVPM** (Università Politecnica delle Marche), Ancona.
* **Frequency:** we typically organize a main physical meeting **monthly**.
* **What to expect:** these meetings focus on hands-on development, specialized talks, and collaborative project planning.

**How to find the next meeting:** specific dates, times, and room details are always announced in advance on our Telegram/Discord channels and on our GitHub repository.
